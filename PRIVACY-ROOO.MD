# ROOO Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 22, 2026

## Key Points

**Data Collection:** ROOO collects minimal information, including contact details when you email support and anonymized usage data to improve the app. The app also uses advertising SDKs (Google AdMob, and Yandex Mobile Ads as a fallback on devices without Google Play Services) that collect your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers to serve and measure ads.

**Advertising & Device Identifiers:** ROOO shows ads to support free use of the app. To do this, we use Google AdMob and, on devices without Google Play Services, Yandex Mobile Ads. These SDKs automatically collect device or other IDs (such as your Advertising ID) and transmit them off your device to Google and/or Yandex for the purpose of serving, personalizing, and measuring ads. This data collection is required to use the ad-supported version of the app and cannot be turned off within the app. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Yandex's Privacy Policy](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/) for details on how each company handles this data.

**Usage:** The app uses collected information to enhance features, respond to user communications, maintain security, and serve advertising. As stated: "We use usage data to understand how people interact with the app so we can improve its performance."

**Sharing:** Personal information is not sold. Device and advertising identifiers are shared with our advertising partners (Google AdMob, Yandex Mobile Ads) as described above. The policy also notes that "anonymized or aggregated data" may be shared with partners, and disclosure may occur if legally required.

**User Control:** Users can decline sharing certain information and may request data deletion by contacting tiralcodes+rooo@gmail.com. Advertising identifier collection is required for the ad-supported version of the app and is not currently user-configurable within the app.

**Security:** The provider implements "security safeguards designed to protect the information we maintain."

**Updates:** Policy changes will be communicated through the app or email notifications.

**Contact:** tiralcodes+rooo@gmail.com
